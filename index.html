DayCount: Days Until and Since

Count down to the dates you're looking forward to, and count up from the ones that matter. Each date is a row with a big number, on screens you can name, color and fill with photos.

Tips

  • Add a date: tap “Add a date” under the rows.
  • Edit: tap a date. Hold it to duplicate it, copy its look or remove it.
  • Styles: in the editor, swipe the preview at the top to try each style. Custom lets you switch parts of the row on or off.
  • Screens: swipe between screens, and tap + under the rows to add one. Tap the screen name for its settings; hold it to reorder screens.
  • Days until and since: each screen counts one way. When a date on a “Days until” screen passes, it moves to a “Days since” screen (you can turn this off in Settings).
