Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

DayCount is made by Andrew Stokes (datadrvn). This policy covers the DayCount app for iPhone.

What the app stores

The dates you add (titles, dates, notes, colors, icons and any photos you add) are saved only on your device and are never sent to us or anyone else. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not collect your name, contacts, location or any identifiers.

Photos

When you add a photo to a date or a screen, you choose it with the system photo picker; the app only receives the photo you pick, resizes it, and stores it on your device.
